# Splizo — Veo video prompts

Rohan and Priya, in 4 parts. Based on a true story, names changed.
Married February 2026, hometown a village, living in the city. Rohan had the
idea; Priya left her job and built it.

**24 clips, ~8s each.** Parts now run 45–50 seconds instead of 32, so each one
is a proper chapter rather than a sketch.

**Generate in Google Flow, Veo 3.1 – Fast, 9:16, x1.** Same chat and project
throughout — the model uses its own earlier clips as a consistency hint.

Veo drifted from part 1's painterly look to a glossier 3D one at the start of part 2. An explicit style anchor was tried and had no effect, so the drift is accepted: part 2 onward is internally consistent, and part 1 is colour-graded at assembly to narrow the gap. Do not spend credits re-rolling for style.

Every prompt is **ready to paste as-is**. Do not re-word the style or character
blocks: that exact wording produced the look in clip 1.1, and changing it is
what breaks consistency.

---

## How the parts hold together

Four things carry across the series, so it reads as one story in instalments
rather than four separate reels:

- **The brown notebook.** New in part 2, a mess by the end of it, slid aside in
  part 2's close, and sitting closed and forgotten on a shelf in part 4.
- **Time moves forward.** February, March, June–October, Dhanteras — each part
  opens with its month on screen.
- **The problem escalates.** Part 1 nobody records anything · part 2 recording
  fails · part 3 it starts costing real money · part 4 solved.
- **Recaps and hooks are added by me at assembly**, not generated. They cost no
  credits and the text stays correctly spelled.
